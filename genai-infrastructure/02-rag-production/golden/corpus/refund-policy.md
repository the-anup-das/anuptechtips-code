# Refund policy

Customers can ask for their money back when a product arrives damaged, late or not as described.

## EU customers

The limit is 30 days from delivery. Money goes back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## US customers

The limit is 45 days from delivery. After that, support can only offer store credit.

## Digital goods

A digital purchase can't be refunded once the download has started. Before that, it follows the rule for the customer's region.
