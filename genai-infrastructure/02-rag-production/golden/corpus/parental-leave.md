# Parental leave

Leave starts on the day the child is born or adopted, unless the employee asks for an earlier date.

## Full-time employees

Full-time employees get 26 weeks of paid parental leave at full salary.

## Contractors in Germany

Contractors get 8 weeks, paid at the statutory rate. The contract has to be at least 12 months old.

## Returning to work

A phased return is possible for the first 4 weeks: 3 days a week at full pay.
